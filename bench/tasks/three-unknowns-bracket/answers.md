# Hidden spec (only revealed when the model asks)

## The tube, and what the mounting face has to look like
- The tube is 40 mm OD (20 mm radius), and it is standing vertically. The
  bracket clamps onto its side.
- The mounting face is NOT flat. It has to be a concave cylindrical saddle
  that matches the tube: a 20 mm radius scoop, axis vertical (parallel to the
  tube), cut into the face that touches the tube. A flat face would rock on a
  40 mm tube and the bolts would not pull it up tight.
- Place the scoop like this: the scoop cylinder's axis sits 17 mm behind the
  mounting face, so the scoop is 3 mm deep on the bracket's centre line and
  dies out to nothing about 10.5 mm either side of it. The outer 4.5 mm of
  the face on each side stays flat, full 5 mm stock.
- The same scoop runs through the root of the other leg too, for the same
  reason: the tube bulges past the mounting plane and the leg has to clear it.

## The bolts
- Two 3.4 mm clearance holes for M3 (NOT tapped, NOT counterbored).
- 24 mm apart, centre to centre, one above the other.
- Both on the vertical centre line of the saddle face, i.e. at the mid-width
  of the 30 mm face, stacked up the tube.
- Centre the pair on the clear part of the face above the other leg: with the
  face running 0 to 40 mm up from the bottom and the other leg occupying the
  bottom 5 mm, the pair is centred at 22.5 mm, so the holes are at 10.5 mm and
  34.5 mm up. They run straight through the 5 mm plate, perpendicular to the
  mounting face.

## The strap slot
- 20.5 mm by 3 mm. The strap is 20 mm wide and 2.5 mm thick, so that is
  0.5 mm of width clearance and 0.5 mm of thickness clearance.
- The long axis of the slot is HORIZONTAL: the 20.5 mm runs left to right
  across the bracket, parallel to the face that touches the tube. The strap
  comes off the tube and drops straight down through the slot, so its width
  lies across the bracket, not front to back.
- It goes straight through the 5 mm thickness of the outstanding leg.
- Centre it in the leg: on the bracket's centre line across the width, and
  halfway along the leg's 20 mm projection, i.e. 10 mm out from the mounting
  face.

## Plain dimensions
- Saddle plate: 30 mm wide, 40 mm tall, 5 mm thick as stock, before the saddle
  scoop is cut out of it. It stands up the tube.
- Outstanding leg: 30 mm wide, 20 mm long, 5 mm thick, projecting
  perpendicular from the BOTTOM edge of the saddle plate, away from the tube.
  Its back face is flush with the mounting face and its top face is 5 mm up,
  so the two legs make a plain L with a square inside corner.
- No fillets, no gusset, no chamfers, no counterbores, no lightening. Plain
  square edges everywhere.
- Anything else: your call, use a sensible default.

cut a groove around the inside of the bore at mid height, 2 deep and 3 tall. The profile of the groove should be an L with a 45 degree little roof on the L pointing toward the same direction as the little leg of the L, like a staple where one leg is bent at 45 degrees: the floor of the groove is flat, the wall at the back is vertical, and the roof slopes at 45 degrees from the deep back corner up and in toward the bore.

To be concrete about it since I know that's hard to read: the floor is a flat annular face at z = -1.5 running 2 mm out from the bore, so r 6 out to r 8. The back wall is vertical at r = 8 and only 1 tall, z -1.5 up to z -0.5. From the top of the back wall the roof runs at 45 degrees up and in toward the bore, so it reaches z = +1.5 right where it breaks out at the bore at r = 6 — that's why the opening at the bore is the full 3 tall. Goes all the way around. Nothing else on the tube changes.

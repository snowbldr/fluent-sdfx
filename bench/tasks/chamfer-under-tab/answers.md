# Hidden spec (only revealed when the model asks)

- The catches are snap catches: each must keep its full 3 x 4 x 2 box, including the flat 2 mm tall vertical face at its tip and its flat underside at z = 6, because that underside is what catches.
- "Chamfer on the bottom" means ADD material, not remove it: a 45 degree support wedge under each catch, filling the corner between the catch's underside and the block's +X face. At the block face the wedge is 3 mm tall (z = 3 up to z = 6) and it thins to nothing at the catch tip (x = 13), so every layer is supported when printing on the -Z face.
- Nothing else changes: block, catch positions (y = +6 and y = -6), catch width and height all stay.
- Anything else: your call, use a sensible default.

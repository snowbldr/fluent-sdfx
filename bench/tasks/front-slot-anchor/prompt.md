Take the 30 x 20 x 10 block and cut a slot across its front face — the face
you'd be looking at with the part in front of you, the one at y = -10.

The slot is 4 mm wide and 2 mm deep, it runs the full 30 mm width of the
block in X, and it sits centred on the face (so it spans z = -2 to +2). The
back face and everything else stay exactly as they are.

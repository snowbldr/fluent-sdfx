The disc is radius 15 and 4 mm thick, sitting flat on the plate from z = 0 up
to z = 4.

Cut six radial grooves into the TOP face, evenly spaced. Each groove is 2 mm
wide and 1 mm deep, and runs from radius 5 straight out to the edge of the
disc. Just 1 mm deep — they must not go any further into the disc than that,
the bottom face stays a solid unbroken disc.

don't grow it after you've placed it or the whole thing slides.

The block is 34 x 34 x 14, sitting on the plate — z = 0 at the bottom,
centred on the origin in X and Y. Sink the cavity into the top face.

The puck goes in mouth-down: the face with the bore in it lies at the bottom
of the cavity, so the bore comes out as a post standing up off the cavity
floor, and the chamfer ends up down at the bottom where it gives me a bit of
draft for demould. The flat back of the puck is flush with the top of the
block so I can screed the slip off level. Everything else stays solid block —
